ABSTRACT

EXTRACT-01 In this work we proposed the Transformer, dispensing with recurrence and convolutions entirely. Experiments show superior quality with significantly less training time.

On the WMT14 English-to-German translation task, the big transformer model improves over the previously best reported results by more than 2.0 BLEU, reaching a new state-of-the-art BLEU score of 28.4.
